# Quantum Ophthalmology Methodology

## Core Technique
**Source**: arXiv:2606.19238 - "Introduction to Quantum Ophthalmology"

## Four Research Directions

### 1. Photon-Limited Retinal Imaging
- Single-photon detection for ultra-low-light retinal imaging
- Exploits human visual system's single-photon sensitivity
- Applications: Early detection of retinal degeneration

### 2. Correlation-Based Imaging
- Quantum correlation techniques for enhanced resolution
- Beyond classical diffraction limits
- Applications: Sub-cellular retinal structure imaging

### 3. Nanoscale Optical Probes
- Quantum dots and NV centers for targeted retinal imaging
- Single-molecule sensitivity
- Applications: Molecular-level disease markers

### 4. Quantum-Limited Visual Perception
- Understanding human vision at quantum limit
- Psychophysical experiments with single photons
- Applications: Novel vision testing paradigms

## Design Principles
1. Human eye is naturally sensitive to single photons
2. Quantum techniques enable sub-diffraction retinal imaging
3. Nanoscale probes enable molecular-level diagnostics
4. Quantum-limited psychophysics provides novel testing methods
