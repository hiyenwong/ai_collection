# Phantom: Physics-Infused Video Generation via Joint Modeling of Visual and Latent Physical Dynamics

## Overview

Recent advances in generative video modeling, driven by large-scale datasets and powerful architectures, have yielded remarkable visual realism. However, emerging evidence suggests that simply scaling data and model size does not endow these systems with an understanding of the underlying physical laws that govern real-world dynamics. Existing approaches often fail to capture or enforce such physical consistency, resulting in unrealistic motion and dynamics.

**Phantom** addresses this gap by integrating the inference of latent physical properties directly into the video generation process. The model jointly models visual content and latent physical dynamics, producing video sequences that are both visually realistic and physically consistent.

## Key Innovation

**Physics-Aware Video Representation**: An abstract yet informative embedding of the underlying physics that:
- Facilitates joint prediction of physical dynamics alongside video content
- Requires no explicit specification of complex physical dynamics and properties
- Enables physically plausible video generation
