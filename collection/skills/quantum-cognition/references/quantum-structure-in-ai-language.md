# Quantum Structure in AI Language

## arXiv:2511.21731 — Identifying Quantum Structure in AI Language: Evidence for Evolutionary Convergence of Human and Artificial Cognition

### Authors
Diederik Aerts, Jonito Aerts Arguëlles, Lester Beltran, Suzette Geriente, Roberto Leporini, Massimiliano Sassoli de Bianchi, Sandro Sozzo

### Journal
Entropy 28, 622, 2026

### Core Findings

#### 1. Bell Inequality Violation in LLMs
- Cognitive tests on conceptual combinations using ChatGPT and Gemini show significant Bell inequality violations
- Probabilities do not satisfy Kolmogorov axioms → non-classical probability model
- Same violation patterns found previously in human cognitive tests
- **Implication**: LLMs exhibit genuine quantum-like structure in meaning representation

#### 2. Bose-Einstein Statistics in LLM Text
- Word distributions in large-size LLM-generated texts follow Bose-Einstein statistics, NOT Maxwell-Boltzmann
- Matches patterns found in human information retrieval tests on large corpora
- **Implication**: Conceptual "particles" (words) in semantic space obey quantum statistical mechanics

#### 3. Evolutionary Convergence Thesis
- Systematic emergence of non-classical quantum-like structures in conceptual-linguistic domains
- Occurs regardless of substrate: human biology (evolution) or LLM (self-learning + training)
- Key hypothesis: meaning-bearing vector spaces built on top of neural networks exhibit the same quantum organization
- Neural network architecture is secondary; the semantic structure is primary

### Methodology
1. **Test 1 (Bell Inequality)**: Present LLMs with conceptual combination tasks, measure response distributions, test for Bell inequality violation
2. **Test 2 (Statistical Distribution)**: Analyze word frequencies in large LLM-generated corpora, fit to Bose-Einstein vs Maxwell-Boltzmann
3. **Synthesis**: Compare results with human cognitive test baselines → evidence for evolutionary convergence

### Connection to Quantum Cognition
This paper extends the quantum cognition paradigm from human cognition to artificial cognition. Human cognition exhibits quantum-like models (Khrennikov framework: contextuality, incompatibility, entanglement-like correlations). LLM cognition exhibits the same quantum structures: Bell violation, Bose-Einstein statistics. The convergence suggests that quantum-like probability is the natural mathematical framework for any system that organizes meaning in vector spaces, regardless of physical substrate.
