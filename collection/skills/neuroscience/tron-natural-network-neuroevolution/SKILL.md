# TRON: Transferring Naturally Evolved Network Architectures into Neuroevolution

## Core Contribution

TRON (TRansfer Of Natural networks) introduces a neuroevolution approach that incorporates organizational principles from naturally evolved biological networks to improve neural architecture search (NAS). Biological networks provide topological priors that support adaptation and evolvability.

## Key Insights

- **Problem**: Evolutionary NAS methods struggle with preserving functional organization during mutation and recombination
- **Solution**: Transfer organizational principles from biological networks to artificial network evolution
- **Hypothesis**: Naturally evolved biological networks contain organizational principles that support adaptation

## Technical Approach

1. **Biological Network Analysis**: Extract topological features from naturally evolved networks
2. **Transfer to Neuroevolution**: Incorporate biological organizational principles into evolutionary operators
3. **Preserved Functional Organization**: Mutation and recombination operators respect biological constraints
4. **NAS Application**: Apply to neural architecture search with improved evolvability

## Benefits

- Better preservation of functional organization during evolution
- Improved evolvability of artificial neural networks
- Bio-inspired topological priors for architecture search
- More effective mutation and recombination operators

## Applications
