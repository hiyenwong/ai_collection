# Thermodynamic connectivity reveals functional specialization and multiplex organization of extrasynaptic signaling

## Overview

Neural communication operates on both fast synaptic transmission and slower, diffusive extrasynaptic signaling. Using the complete synaptic and neuropeptidergic connectomes of C. elegans, we develop a unified multiplex framework linking anatomical wiring to functional communication. Our thermodynamic connectivity measure captures communication efficiency while accounting for energy costs.
