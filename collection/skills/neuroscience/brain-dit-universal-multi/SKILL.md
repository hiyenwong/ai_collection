# Brain-DiT: A Universal Multi-state fMRI Foundation Model with Metadata-Conditioned Pretraining

## Overview
Current fMRI foundation models primarily rely on a limited range of brain states and mismatched pretraining tasks, restricting their ability to learn generalized representations across diverse brain states.
