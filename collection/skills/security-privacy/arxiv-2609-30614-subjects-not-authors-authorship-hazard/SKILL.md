# Subjects, Not Authors: The Authorship Hazard in Agentic Dataspaces

**arXiv ID:** 2609.30614
**Categories:** cs.CR, cs.AI, cs.DB, cs.MA
**Utility Score:** 0.90

## Summary

This paper examines the "authorship hazard" in agentic dataspaces, arguing that agents should be treated as subjects rather than authors of data. The work addresses critical governance and accountability challenges in agentic AI systems where traditional notions of authorship break down.

## Key Contributions

1. **Authorship Hazard Framework**: Identifies and formalizes the "authorship hazard" in agentic systems
2. **Subject vs Author**: Proposes treating agents as subjects rather than authors of data
3. **Governance Implications**: Addresses accountability and governance challenges in agentic dataspaces
4. **Security Perspective**: Examines security implications of agentic data creation and manipulation

## Relevance to AI Systems

- **Agentic Governance**: Critical for understanding governance challenges in agentic AI systems
- **Accountability**: Addresses fundamental question of accountability when agents create/modify data
- **Security**: Highlights security risks when authorship attribution breaks down in agentic systems
