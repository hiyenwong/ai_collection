# OverAct: Measuring and Mitigating Proactive Over-Authorization in LLM Tool-Calling Agents

**arXiv:** [2610.01508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01508)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**Categories:** cs.AI; cs.CR; cs.CL
**Utility Score:** 0.86

## Abstract

LLM agents with tool-calling capabilities can access external services and private user data, but they may retrieve more information than a users request explicitly requires. We study this behavior and term it proactive over-authorization. We introduce OverAct, a controlled benchmark spanning eight privacy-sensitive domains with deterministic, judge-free scoring. Across seven models from four families, all models significantly exceed authorized scope. We propose SelfAudit, a zero-shot inference-time method that generates request-grounded justifications and filters unjustified calls before execution, reducing privacy-oriented excess by 43%.
