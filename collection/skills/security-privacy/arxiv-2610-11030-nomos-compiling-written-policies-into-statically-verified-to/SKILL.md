# NOMOS: Compiling Written Policies into Statically Verified Tool-Call Gates for LLM Agents

**arXiv:** [2610.11030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11030)
**Authors:** Anonymous
**Categories:** cs.AI; cs.CR; cs.SE
**Utility Score:** 0.89

## Abstract

Tool-using LLM agents violate the policies they are deployed to enforce, often silently. NOMOS, a four-pass compiler, turns a natural-language policy into a deterministic tool-call gate; static verification with tool-schema-level checks alone repairs or rejects 37% (airline) and 13% (retail) of candidates. On tau-bench the gate cuts violations of reference-encoded clauses among state-changing calls from 66.3% to 2.6% (airline) and 30.8% to 6.9% (retail). Unlike AgentDojo shipped defenses, it reaches a zero attack success rate on banking. Decisions take microseconds without an LLM call.
