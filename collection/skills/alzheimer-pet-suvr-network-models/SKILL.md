# High-Fidelity Network-Based Models of Alzheimer Disease Progression

## Overview
Mathematical models of Alzheimer disease progression modeling amyloid-beta and tau protein misfolding and accumulation on brain network structures. Validated against PET-SUVR imaging data.

Based on: [High-fidelity and Network-based Spatio-temporal Mathematical Models of Alzheimer Disease Progression and their Validation Against PET-SUVR Imaging Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18470) (2026-04-20)

## Key Insights
- Models amyloid-beta and tau protein accumulation dynamics
- Network-based spatio-temporal modeling
- Validated against PET-SUVR imaging data
- Quantitative tool for disease progression monitoring

## Applications
- Alzheimer research
- Clinical trial design
- Biomarker development
- Disease progression modeling

## Abstract
We develop high-fidelity spatio-temporal mathematical models of Alzheimer disease progression, modeling amyloid-beta and tau protein misfolding and accumulation on brain network structures. The models are validated against PET-SUVR imaging data, providing a quantitative framework for disease progression monitoring and clinical trial design.

## Methodology

### Network-Based Spatio-Temporal Modeling
- Model protein misfolding (Abeta, tau) on brain networks
- Spatio-temporal dynamics validated against PET-SUVR data
- Quantitative framework for disease progression
