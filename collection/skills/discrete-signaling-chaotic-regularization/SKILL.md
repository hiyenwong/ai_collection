# Discrete Signaling Chaotic Regularization

## Overview
Methodology from arXiv:2606.04426 (submitted 3 Jun 2026) that links microscopic chaos in recurrent neural networks to macroscopic geometry of neural representations.

**Core Discovery**: Chaotic dynamics induce local roughness (sharp distortions at small scales) while preserving global smoothness across larger stimulus variations, acting as an intrinsic regularizer that enhances generalization while maintaining expressivity.

## Pitfalls & Edge Cases

### Common Mistakes
1. **Assuming Smoothness**: Don't expect local smoothness - chaos induces roughness
2. **Global vs Local**: Distinguish between small-scale and large-scale behavior
3. **Spectral Misinterpretation**: Power-law signatures indicate chaos, not pathology

### Edge Cases
- **Weak Chaos**: Regularization may be insufficient
- **Strong Chaos**: May overwhelm global smoothness
- **Mixed Regimes**: Combine chaotic and stable dynamics
