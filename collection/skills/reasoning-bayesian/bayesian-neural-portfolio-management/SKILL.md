# Robust investment portfolio management for dynamic financial markets using Bayesian neural networks

## Overview
Bayesian neural network methodology for robust portfolio management in dynamic financial markets. Uses Bayesian inference to quantify uncertainty in portfolio optimization, adapt to changing market conditions, and provide probabilistic risk assessments. Use when building portfolio management systems with uncertainty quantification, dynamic market adaptation, or probabilistic risk modeling.
