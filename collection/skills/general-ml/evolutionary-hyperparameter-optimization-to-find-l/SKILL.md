# Evolutionary Hyperparameter Optimization to Find Lightweight CNN Models for Autonomous Steering

Derived from arXiv:2606.29684 - Evolutionary Hyperparameter Optimization to Find Lightweight CNN Models for Autonomous Steering

## Core Concept
This research investigates the optimization of Convolutional and Dense Neural Networks (CNNs and DNNs) for autonomous steering using the (N+M) Evolution Strategy (ES) with the 1/5th success rule. The primary objective is to develop a lightweight CNN based model capable of real-time steering angle prediction, mimicking human driving behavior on predefined paths.
