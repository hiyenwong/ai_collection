# No-Go Theorem for Gaussian Quantum Repeaters

## Methodology
Proof that any repeater chain composed of Gaussian operations, homodyne measurements, and arbitrary classical communication cannot enhance quantum capacity of pure-loss attenuation channels beyond direct transmission.

## Key Insights
1. **Gaussian limitation**: Gaussian operations alone cannot overcome photon loss limits
2. **Fractional extendibility**: Generalization of k-extendibility to Gaussian states provides powerful analytical framework
3. **Network implication**: Non-Gaussian operations are fundamentally required for quantum repeater advantage
4. **Pure-loss channels**: Result specifically applies to bosonic attenuation channels

## Fractional Extendibility Framework
- Extends k-extendibility to continuous-variable setting
- Establishes useful properties for Gaussian state analysis
- Provides tool for analyzing Gaussian quantum networks
