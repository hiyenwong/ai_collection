## New Papers Discovered

### arXiv:2507.10722 — Bridging Brains and Machines
- **Type**: Position & Survey Paper
- **Authors**: Sohan Shankar, Yi Pan, Hanqi Jiang, Zhengliang Liu, et al. (50+ authors)
- **Categories**: q-bio.NC, cs.NE
- **Summary**: Identifies convergence of neuroscience, AGI, and neuromorphic computing. Maps brain mechanisms to AI architectures: synaptic plasticity→fine-tuning, sparse spike-based communication→sparse attention, multimodal association→foundation models. Discusses physical substrates: memristive crossbars, in-memory compute arrays, quantum and photonic devices. Four critical challenges: (1) spiking dynamics + foundation models, (2) lifelong plasticity without catastrophic forgetting, (3) unified language + sensorimotor learning, (4) ethical safeguards in neuromorphic autonomous systems.
- **Key insight**: 50+ author consensus on unified research paradigm; excellent survey of neuroscience→AI→hardware trajectory.

### arXiv:2408.14221 — Brain Functions as Thermal Equilibrium States
- **Type**: Research Paper (published in Physical Review Research)
- **Authors**: Elkaïoum M. Moutuou, Habib Benali
- **Categories**: q-bio.NC, cond-mat.dis-nn, cond-mat.stat-mech, math.OA, quant-ph
- **DOI**: 10.1103/jmqh-bqnc
- **Summary**: Algebraic quantum model bridging connectome structure to function. Maps anatomical connectome (directed multigraph) to graph algebra (C*-algebra). Brain functions identified as KMS (Kubo-Martin-Schwinger) thermal equilibrium states. Features: (1) functional connectome from topology, (2) Integration Capacity (IC) index for neuronal coordination. Validated on C. elegans (302 neurons).
- **Key insight**: Structure-function relationship via algebraic quantum formalism, not correlation-based FC.

### arXiv:2406.16991 — Emergent Schrödinger Equation for Single Neurons
- **Type**: Theoretical Paper
- **Authors**: Partha Ghose
- **Categories**: q-bio.NC, quant-ph
- **Summary**: Electrical noise (Brownian motion) in neuron membranes → emergent Schrödinger equation with new neuronal constant. Challenges view that QM is irrelevant to macroscopic biological systems. Empirical test: look for quantum fluctuations in subthreshold neural oscillations.
- **Key insight**: Noise creates, not destroys, quantum-like behavior at the neuronal level.

### arXiv:2405.02370 — Neuromorphic Correlates of Artificial Consciousness
- **Type**: Theoretical Framework
- **Authors**: Anwaar Ulhaq
- **Categories**: cs.AI, eess.SP
- **Summary**: NCAC framework extending NCC concept to artificial systems. Merges neuromorphic design with brain simulations. Design principles: event-driven processing, recurrent feedback, multi-scale integration, embodied interaction. Informed by Human Brain Project, EEG/fMRI, IIT.
- **Key insight**: Consciousness correlates can be designed into neuromorphic architectures, not just observed post-hoc.
