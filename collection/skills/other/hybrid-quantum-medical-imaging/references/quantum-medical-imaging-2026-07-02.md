# Quantum Medical Imaging Research Notes (2026-07-02)

## Key Papers

### 1. First-in-Human Quantum Entanglement Imaging (arXiv:2606.29421)
- **Authors**: Moskal, Kumar, Sharma, Beyene, Chug
- **Published**: 2026-06-28
- **Scanner**: J-PET (Jagiellonian Positron Emission Tomography) - plastic scintillators
- **Radiopharmaceutical**: 68Ga-DOTA-TATE
- **Measurement**: Compton effect provides position + time + polarization plane
- **Finding**: Entanglement degree for liver/spleen between separable and maximally entangled
- **Significance**: First clinical exploitation of quantum entanglement in diagnostics

### 2. PET with Quantum-Entangled Compton Events (arXiv:2606.25804)
- **Authors**: Makek, Kozuljevic, Bokulic, Grosev, Kuncic
- **Published**: 2026-06-24
- **Setup**: Single-layer scintillator polarimeters
- **Sources**: 68Ge line sources (45 MBq each), NEMA NU-4 phantom (378 MBq)
- **Results**: 2.5mm spatial resolution (single-pixel), 3.6-4.9mm (polarization-correlated), 20% higher SBR, 10% sensitivity increase

### 3. Compression-Driven Anomaly Detection in Brain MRI Using QAE (arXiv:2606.27411)
- **Authors**: Ganguly, Liang, Makris
- **Published**: 2026-06-25
- **Architecture**: Angle encoding → variational encoder-decoder → trash qubits
- **Performance**: Slice-level ROC-AUC ~0.95, patch-level ~0.813
- **Key Finding**: Encoder's structured compression drives detection, not decoder expressivity

### 4. CV Photonic QNN for Edge Quantum AI (arXiv:2606.28252)
- **Authors**: Sonawane, Choe, Tamil
- **Published**: 2026-06-26
- **Pipeline**: MobileNetV1 → PCA (16D) → CV-QNN (Φ∘D∘U₁)
- **Results**: 18 params, 100% calibrated test accuracy, exceeds 55-param classical baseline
- **Barren Plateau Mitigation**: PCA + encoding restriction raises gradient variance 58 orders of magnitude

### 5. Introduction to Quantum Ophthalmology (arXiv:2606.19238)
- **Authors**: Kulmaganbetov, Pushin, Singh, Chahal, Cory
- **Published**: 2026-06-17
- **Four Directions**: Photon-limited retinal imaging, ghost imaging, quantum dot probes, quantum-limited vision
