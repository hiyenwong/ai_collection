# Quantum Medicine Knowledge Bank (2026-06-24)

## Quantum Ophthalmology (arXiv:2606.19238)
**Authors**: Kulmaganbetov, Pushin, Singh (2026-06-17)
**Core contribution**: First comprehensive survey defining quantum ophthalmology as a distinct subfield with four directions:
1. Photon-limited retinal imaging (OCT with single-photon detection, reduced phototoxicity)
2. Correlation-based imaging (quantum correlation enhanced contrast, reduced speckle noise)
3. Nanoscale optical probes (quantum dots, single-molecule sensitivity, in vivo retinal labeling)
4. Quantum-limited visual perception (psychophysical thresholds for photon detection)

**Clinical significance**: Reduces phototoxicity for pediatric/long-term imaging; enables cellular-level resolution

## Quantum GAN Medical Imaging Benchmark (arXiv:2606.18970)
**Authors**: Haider, Figini (2026-06-17)
**Core contribution**: Rigorous controlled benchmark of quantum-Latent GAN for brain MRI classification
- Parameter-matched WGAN-GP comparison (quantum vs classical generators)
- Multi-seed significance testing across data fractions
- **Key finding**: NO significant advantage of quantum generators across data regimes
- Low-data benefit is regularization effect, NOT faithful data expansion
- Mode collapse diagnostics showed similar behavior

**Warning**: Many quantum medical imaging papers claim accuracy gains without parameter-matching or multi-seed testing

## Correlation Aware Quantum Feature Map (arXiv:2606.21570)
**Authors**: Murat Kurt (2026-06-19)
**Core contribution**: CAQFM — integrates classical feature correlations into quantum encoding
- Uses Pearson, Spearman, Kendall Tau, Mutual Information, Distance Correlation
- Constructs entanglement structures reflecting actual data correlations
- Spearman/Kendall variants achieved highest performance on breast cancer diagnosis benchmarks
- **Application**: Medical classification tasks (breast cancer, Alzheimer)
