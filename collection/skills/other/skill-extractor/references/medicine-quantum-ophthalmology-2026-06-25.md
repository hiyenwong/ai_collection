## Quantum Ophthalmology Sub-Domain (2026-06-25 verified)

Paper: **2606.19238** "Introduction to Quantum Ophthalmology" — first systematic review of quantum technologies in ophthalmology.

### Four Pillars
1. **Photon-limited retinal imaging**: Single-photon detection (SPAD + OCT) reduces phototoxicity while preserving diagnostic image quality
2. **Correlation-based ghost imaging**: Alternative image formation through scattering ocular media (cataract, corneal opacity)
3. **Quantum dot nanoscale probes**: Tunable, photostable contrast agents for targeted retinal layer imaging (RPE, photoreceptors)
4. **Single-photon visual perception**: Human retina detects single photons with above-chance reliability — probes rod cell quantum efficiency
