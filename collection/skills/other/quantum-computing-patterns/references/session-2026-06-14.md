### arXiv:2606.11392 - CoMPuTE
**Full title**: "Compressed minimum-purity time evolution for late-time quantum dynamics"
**Authors**: Moksh Bhateja, Jonas B. Rigo, Markus Schmitt
**Categories**: cond-mat.stat-mech, quant-ph

**Key contribution**: Addresses entanglement growth bottleneck in many-body dynamics simulation.
CoMPuTE tracks consistent reduced local density matrices using minimum-purity principle to
close the hierarchy of equations of motion.

**Benchmarks**:

- (ii) Out-of-equilibrium Floquet dynamics from pure state — applicable
