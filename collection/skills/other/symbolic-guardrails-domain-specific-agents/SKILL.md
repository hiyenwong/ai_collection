# Symbolic Guardrails for Domain-Specific Agents: Stronger Safety and Security Guarantees Without Sacrificing Utility

**arXiv ID:** 2604.16229  
**Published:** 2026-04-18  
**Authors:** Yining Hong, Yining She, Eunsuk Kang  
**Categories:** cs.SE, cs.AI, cs.CR  
**Utility Score:** 1.0

## Abstract

AI agents that interact with their environments through tools enable powerful applications, but in high-stakes business settings, unintended actions can cause unacceptable harm, such as privacy breaches and financial loss. Existing mitigations, such as guardrails that check whether an agent's actions satisfy a policy, can be bypassed by adversarial attacks that exploit the gap between an agent's reasoning and the guardrail's understanding of the agent's actions.

## Key Contributions

1. **Symbolic Guardrails**: Novel approach to agent safety using symbolic reasoning
2. **Adversarial Robustness**: Addresses bypass attacks on traditional guardrails
3. **Domain-Specific Safety**: Tailored safety guarantees for specific application domains
