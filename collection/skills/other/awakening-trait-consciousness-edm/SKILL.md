# Awakening as a Trait-Level Transformation of Consciousness (arXiv 2610.20856)

**Paper**: Ihalainen, Oswald, Kumar, Major, Subramaniam, Laureys, Tewarie (Laval/CERVO + Liège + Harvard + Nottingham), q-bio.NC, 14 Oct 2026.

## When to Use

Use when studying: meditation/neuroscience of self, trait vs state measures in contemplative neuroscience, dereification constructs, nondual awareness, distinguishing adaptive contemplative change from dissociation/ego-dissolution pathologies, or designing studies of long-term practitioners.

## Core Thesis

Awakening ≠ a transient altered state (meditation absorption, psychedelic peak, nondual episode). It is a **trait-level reorganization of the global STRUCTURE of experience** — durable change in how contents are related to, not in what contents appear. Formally: a transformation of the state DISTRIBUTION, not of any single state.

Two complementary distribution properties define the trait:
1. **Stabilized dereified baseline** — mean shift toward reduced identification with mental contents;
2. **Dynamical flexibility** — lowered transition cost between reified (action-oriented) and dereified (integrative) modes.

## Four Functional Dimensions (Table 1 of paper)

| Dimension | Reified mode (ordinary) | Dereified mode (awakened) | Neural markers (evidence strength) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self–content coupling | thoughts appropriated as "me/mine" | seen as transient mental events; decentered | narrative/expressional self decoupling, mPFC/PCC deactivation, reduced posterior-DMN connectivity (convergent) |
| Affective reactivity | perception→emotion escalation | lower reactivity, faster recovery, equanimity | mixed/indirect |
| Temporal organization | narrative past/future projection | present-centred, reduced mental time-travel | DMN topographic centrality & intrinsic timescales (theoretical) |
| Self-referential evaluation | continuous self-monitoring/judgment | loosened evaluative grip | mPFC/PCC reduction incl. trait-level at rest (convergent) |

## The Enactive-Dereification Model (EDM)

Awakening = graded movement along a **dereification gradient**: from mutual-identification mode (subject/object reified as independent entities) toward relational co-emergence mode (nested context/body/self spheres with porous boundaries). Rooted in Lutz's phenomenological matrix (dereification as metacognitive dimension reducing "experiential coupling") and Dorjee's Dereification Gradient.

Mechanism narrative: dereification destabilizes the "solid stream" (discontinuity moments) → reorganization under relational principles → stabilization of the dereified mode as trait. Nondual awareness (NDA) is the apex of the gradient but **cannot be a stable endpoint** — total ego-dissolution would break egocentric action/homeostatic orientation. Awakening = reorganization that PRESERVES functional self-related processing while loosening automatic identification. Both extremes of the gradient are non-functional/potentially pathological.

## Key Methodological Contributions

1. **Operational definition** separating: transient states (should fade off-cushion) vs trait (persists at rest); awakening (flexible reorganization, preserved integration) vs depersonalization/dissociation (loss of structure, impaired integration) vs psychopathological ego-dissolution.
2. **State-to-trait problem** (Section 5.2): most meditation neuroscience measures states during practice; trait-level claims need resting-state signatures tracked to independently assessed transformation, not practice hours.
3. **Falsifiable predictions**: (a) reduced dwell time in self-related network configurations at rest; (b) increased flexibility of transitions across large-scale networks (salience-network-mediated switching); (c) altered intrinsic timescale organization in posterior medial cortex/DMN; (d) these must track assessed trait transformation, NOT cumulative practice hours — the model is weakened if signatures appear only during practice or track dosage.

## Honest Evidence Assessment (paper's own)

- Convergent: self–content coupling and self-referential evaluation (mPFC/PCC findings replicated).
- Mixed/indirect: affective reactivity.
- Theoretical only: temporal organization (Northoff world-brain relation; DMN timescales).
- No micro-phenomenological study of STABILIZED awakening exists yet; "awakening-like" reports (PNSE, Taylor) are drawn from studies not designed around this construct.

## Reusable Patterns

1. **Distribution-over-states trait definition**: define psychological traits as durable changes in the distribution (and transition costs) of momentary states — applicable to any longitudinal contemplative/affective study design.
2. **Content vs structure separation**: ask whether a transformation adds content or reorganizes structure; this dictates which paradigms can detect it (content paradigms under-detect structural change).
3. **Trait-vs-state-dosage discriminant**: a neural signature only counts as trait evidence if it (i) persists at rest, (ii) correlates with independently assessed transformation, and (iii) does NOT reduce to practice hours — a three-way test usable in any meditation-science design.
4. **Gradient endpoints pathology check**: both extremes of any contemplative gradient are non-functional; design biomarker studies to sample the middle, flexible regime.

## Limitations to Keep in Mind

- Purposive (not systematic) literature selection; scriptural sources read functionally, as hypothesis-generating.
- EDM is a conceptual synthesis — no direct empirical test yet; predictions are at the level of resting-state dynamics.
- Buddhist/mindfulness-weighted evidence base; other contemplative traditions under-represented.
