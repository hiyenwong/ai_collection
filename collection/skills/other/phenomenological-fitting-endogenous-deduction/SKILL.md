# From Phenomenological Fitting to Endogenous Deduction: A Paradigm Leap via Meta-Principle Physics Architecture

## Overview

This paper proposes a paradigm leap from pure phenomenological fitting to the fusion of phenomenological fitting and endogenous deduction. By embedding physical meta-principles into neural network architecture, we construct the Meta-Principle Physics Architecture (MPPA).
