# Hot-Start Quantum Portfolio Optimization

## Key Paper
- Hot-Starting Quantum Portfolio Optimization (arXiv: 2510.11153)
- Quantum Portfolio Optimization: An Extensive Benchmark (arXiv: 2509.17876)
- Quantum Computing for Financial Transformation (arXiv: 2604.08180)

## Core Insight
Classical smooth optimization solutions can initialize quantum portfolio algorithms, constraining the search space to a neighborhood of the classical optimum. This significantly reduces the number of qubits needed and improves convergence rates.

## Application
- Discrete mean-variance portfolio optimization
- Assets must be traded in integer quantities
- QUBO formulation benefits from hot-start initialization
- Search space reduction from classical warm-start
