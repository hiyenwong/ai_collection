# Action Potentials and Solitons

## Overview
This methodology explores the connection between soliton theory and action potential propagation in neurons. Over the past decade, soliton concepts have become increasingly important for understanding the physics of nerve pulse propagation, providing a framework that complements traditional Hodgkin-Huxley models with insights from nonlinear wave dynamics.

## Key Concepts

### Soliton Theory in Neuroscience
- Solitons are stable, localized wave packets that maintain their shape while propagating
- In neural contexts, solitons represent self-reinforcing action potential waves
- The framework provides physical insights into pulse stability and propagation speed

### Nerve Pulse Propagation Physics
- Action potentials as nonlinear solitary waves
- Energy conservation and dissipation in pulse propagation  
- Mechanical and electrical coupling in nerve fibers

### Mathematical Framework
- Nonlinear partial differential equations describing pulse dynamics
- Soliton solutions to nerve equation models
- Stability analysis of propagating pulses

## Applications

### Computational Neuroscience
