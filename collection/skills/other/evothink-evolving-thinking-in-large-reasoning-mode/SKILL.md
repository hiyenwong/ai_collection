## Key Innovation
Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) often suffer from overthinking due to redundant verification steps. Existing approaches for mitigating overthinking, such as fast-slow thinking switching and reasoning trajectory compression, fail to make a fine-grained distinction between beneficial and redundant steps within the LRM's reasoning process, and may thus impair reasoning capability in their pursuit of efficiency.
