## Key Innovation
Smartphone personal assistants reason over longitudinal personal data, yet evaluating them requires context-rich evaluation data whose correct answers are known, and real device traces are too privacy-sensitive to share. To address this challenge, we present SenWorld, a physically grounded, deterministic, event-sourced digital-twin simulation that generates such data with ground truth fixed by construction.
