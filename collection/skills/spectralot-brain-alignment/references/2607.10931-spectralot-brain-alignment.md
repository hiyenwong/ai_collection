# SpectralOT Brain Alignment Paper Reference

## Paper Information
- **Title**: Fast Whole-Brain, Geometry-Aware Functional Alignment for Cross-Subject Decoding
- **Authors**: Pierre-Luc Barbarant, Florian Meyniel, Bertrand Thirion
- **Date**: July 15, 2026
- **arXiv ID**: 2607.10931v1
- **Categories**: q-bio.NC
- **PDF**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.10931.pdf
- **Abstract**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10931

## Key Contributions
1. Introduces Spectral Optimal Transport (SpectralOT) method for fMRI data alignment
2. Embeds cortical geometry into Laplace-Beltrami eigenmodes to regularize alignment
3. Balances functional alignment with anatomical preservation
4. Orders of magnitude faster than state-of-the-art methods
5. Enables population-scale cross-subject decoding studies

## Method Overview
- Computes Laplace-Beltrami eigenmodes from cortical surfaces to encode geometric information
- Projects fMRI data onto these geometric eigenmodes
- Applies optimal transport in the embedded space to align functional data
- Uses geometric embedding as regularizer to prevent over-alignment
