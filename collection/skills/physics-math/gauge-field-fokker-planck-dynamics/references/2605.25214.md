# Quantum-Analogue Formalism for Supraliminal Information Processing

## Paper: arXiv:2605.25214
**Title**: A Quantum-Analogue Formalism for Modeling Supraliminal Information Processing
**Author**: Ihor Lubashevsky
**Published**: 2026-05-24
**Category**: q-bio.NC

## Summary

Develops a novel cloud-function formalism describing the dynamical relationship between sensory-information processing in large-scale brain networks (supraliminal processing) and the content of the mental representation of an observed object. The formalism combines:

1. Neural field theory for large-scale neural activity
2. Spatial characteristics of perceived objects
3. First-person perspective embedding in environment

## Key Features

- **Cloud function**: Characterized by spatial structure inheriting properties of perceived physical object
- **Temporal evolution**: Governed by regularities reflecting intrinsic properties of large-scale neural activity
- **Governing equation**: Neural-field model with polynomial nonlinearities and global phase-shift invariance
- **Interpretation**: Schrödinger-type equation with nonlinear non-Hermitian Hamiltonian
- **Lotka-Volterra terms**: Additional terms analogous to population dynamics models

## Application

Applied to the change-of-mind phenomenon in decision-making:
- Initial choice may be revised during execution
- Explained as interplay between fast preconscious sensory processing and slower conscious comparison
- Consistent with neurophysiological evidence for continuous post-decisional evidence accumulation
- Cloud-function self-interaction discussed as necessary component
