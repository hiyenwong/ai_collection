# Claude's Mathematical Capabilities: Riemann Zeta Function Research

## Overview
This methodology describes how an unreleased research version of Claude made significant strides on a problem related to the Riemann hypothesis by improving a longstanding lower bound for the fraction of zeros of the Riemann zeta function that satisfy the hypothesis, increasing it from 41.6% to 67.2%.

- **AI-Assisted Mathematical Research**: Demonstrates the capability of advanced language models to contribute to deep mathematical problems
