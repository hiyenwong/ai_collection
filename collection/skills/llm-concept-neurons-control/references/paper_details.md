## Abstract
This paper investigates psychological concept neurons in LLMs using the Big Five personality framework. Through probing analysis, it identifies neurons selective for specific psychological constructs and demonstrates control through activation intervention. The study reveals a gap between representation control (probe readability) and behavior control (label generation).

## Key Contributions
1. Identification of Big Five concept neurons in LLMs
2. Causal intervention methodology for neural control
3. Quantification of representation-behavior control gap
