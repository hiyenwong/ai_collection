# Ultra-Fast Language Generation via Discrete Diffusion Divergence Instruct

## Abstract

We introduce DiDi-Instruct, a training-based method that initializes from a pre-trained diffusion LLM and distills a few-step student for fast generation. The model matches or surpasses its dLLM teacher and GPT-2 baseline while providing up to 64x acceleration. Based on integral KL-divergence minimization with grouped reward normalization and reward-guided ancestral sampler. Achieves perplexity 62.2 (8 NFEs) to 18.4 (128 NFEs) on OpenWebText.
