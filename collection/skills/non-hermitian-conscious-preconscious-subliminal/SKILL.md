# Non-Hermitian Potential Well Formalism for Conscious--Preconscious--Subliminal Processing

## Core Contribution

Phenomenological model of the Global Neuronal Workspace (GNW) that uses **non-Hermitian quantum mechanics** to unify sensory encoding and conscious access within a single dynamical framework.

## Key Methodology

### Complex-Valued Landscape
- Early sensory processing generates an effective **complex-valued landscape**
- Governs dynamics of high-level stimulus representations
- Provides dynamical bridge between sensory encoding and conscious access

### Cloud Function on Hilbert Space
- High-level representations encoded in a **cloud function** defined on Hilbert space over perceptual state space
- Combines holistic structure of mental images with neural implementation
- Dynamics governed by **nonlinear Schrodinger-type equation** in imaginary time

### Non-Hermitian Hamiltonian
- **Hermitian part**: Recognition via dissipative localization at minima of GNW landscape
- **Anti-Hermitian part**: Information broadcasting via spatial spreading across state space
- Non-normal Hamiltonian with nonlinear Lotka-Volterra-type term preserving norm
- Enables spatially nonlocal interactions

### Consciousness Hierarchy
The dynamics reproduces three-level hierarchy:
1. **Subliminal processing**: Below threshold, no bound state
2. **Preconscious processing**: Intermediate dynamics
3. **Conscious access**: Emergence of bound state when BOTH:
   - GNW landscape depth exceeds threshold
   - Top-down attention exceeds threshold

## Theoretical Significance

- First tractable dynamical description linking sensory processing, attention, and conscious access
- Bridges quantum formalism with neuroscience without claiming quantum effects in brain
- Provides mathematical framework for testing consciousness theories
- Unifies recognition (localization) and broadcasting (spreading) in single equation

## Potential Applications

- Computational modeling of consciousness disorders
- Attention mechanism design for AI systems
- Theoretical framework for GNW experimental validation
- Bridge between physics and neuroscience consciousness research

## Limitations

- Phenomenological model - not derived from first principles
- Requires experimental validation of threshold predictions
- Complex-valued landscape is mathematical construct, not physical measurement
- Imaginary time Schrodinger equation is analogy, not literal quantum dynamics
