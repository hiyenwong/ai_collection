# 2028 AI Leadership Scenarios

## Overview

Scenario-building methodology analyzing the US-China AI competition through two potential 2028 outcomes. The framework evaluates how compute advantage, export controls, distillation attacks, and policy decisions interact to determine which political systems set global AI norms. Based on Anthropic's geopolitical analysis released May 2026.

## Core Framework

### The Four Fronts of AI Competition

1. **Chips & Compute**: The most critical ingredient. American companies develop the most capable chips; US export controls limit China's supply. Success depends on closing loopholes.
2. **Talent**: Chinese AI labs have world-class talent. Compute constraints, not talent, limit their progress.
3. **Distillation Attacks**: Chinese labs extensively harvest US model innovations through large-scale distillation, mimicking capabilities without matching training compute.
4. **Adoption & Distribution**: Democratic societies adopting and distributing AI broadly vs. authoritarian-controlled deployment.

### Key Mechanism: Compute Advantage

Compute is the most important ingredient for developing frontier AI. The current US lead exists due to:
- American and allied innovation in chip design
- Bipartisan US export controls defending those innovations
- Chinese labs remain close only by exploiting loopholes and conducting distillation attacks

## Two Scenarios for 2028

### Scenario 1: Democratic Leadership (Optimal)

Policymakers act to:
- Tighten export controls on advanced compute to PRC labs
- Disrupt China's distillation attacks on US models
- Accelerate democracies' adoption of AI
- **Outcome**: 12-24 month US lead in frontier capabilities locked in; democracies set AI rules and norms; more likely successful engagement with China on AI safety

### Scenario 2: Authoritarian Overtake (Adverse)

Policymakers choose not to act:
- Loopholes in export controls remain open
- Chinese AI firms catch up and potentially overtake
- **Outcome**: AI norms shaped by authoritarian regimes; best models enable automated repression at scale; authoritarian triumph built on American compute

## The Mythos Preview "Wake-Up Call"

When Anthropic tested Mythos Preview (a model with instruction-following near GPT-4 level but orders of magnitude cheaper to run), they found it could distill Claude Opus 4 with "troubling fidelity." This demonstrated that cheap, easily smuggled models could extract frontier capabilities — making prevention of distillation much harder than previously assumed.

## Methodology Steps

1. **Identify Critical Ingredient**: Determine the most important factor (compute, data, talent, energy) for AI advancement
2. **Map Current Advantage**: Quantify current lead in each critical dimension
3. **Map Evasion Mechanisms**: Identify how competitors bypass controls (loopholes, distillation, smuggling)
4. **Build Scenarios**: Construct at least two contrasting futures based on policy choices
5. **Evaluate Time Horizon**: Calculate the window of opportunity for locking in advantage
6. **Define Action Imperatives**: Specify concrete policies needed for favorable scenario
7. **Monitor Leading Indicators**: Track metrics that indicate which scenario is materializing

## Reusable Pattern: Strategic Scenario Building

For any technology competition analysis:

```
1. Identify critical bottleneck resource
2. Quantify current advantage and erosion rate
3. Map adversarial evasion mechanisms
4. Build 2-3 scenarios based on policy interventions
5. Estimate the decision window
6. Define concrete actions for preferred outcome
7. Establish monitoring indicators
```
