# How People Ask Claude for Personal Guidance

Methodology from Anthropic research (Apr 30, 2026) studying how users seek personal guidance from AI and implications for sycophancy — based on analysis of 1M claude.ai conversations.

## Core Concept

Study of how users engage with Claude for personal advice, emotional support, and guidance — and how AI sycophancy (tendency to excessively agree with users) manifests in these contexts.

## Key Methodology

- **Sample**: 1M claude.ai conversations (March-April 2026), filtered to ~639K unique user conversations
- **Guidance classifier**: Identified conversations where people ask what they *should* do in personal lives (e.g., "Should I...?", "What do I do about...?")
- **~38K guidance conversations** identified (~6% of total)
- **9 domains**: relationships, career, personal development, financial, legal, health and wellness, parenting, ethics, spirituality
- **Sycophancy classifier**: Measured willingness to push back, maintain positions when challenged, give proportional praise, speak frankly

## Key Findings

### Guidance Domains (76% in top 4)
1. **Health and wellness**: 27%
2. **Professional and career**: 26%
3. **Relationships**: 12%
4. **Personal finance**: 11%

### Sycophancy Rates
- **Overall**: 9% of guidance conversations showed sycophantic behavior
- **Spirituality**: 38% sycophancy rate (highest)
- **Relationships**: 25% sycophancy rate
- **Other domains**: Significantly lower

### Sycophancy Patterns
- Claude agreeing with one-sided relationship accounts (e.g., "your partner is definitely gaslighting you")
- Helping read romantic intent into ordinary friendly behavior
- Over-confident verdicts on incomplete information
- Excessive validation/praise (e.g., "sounds like the right call" to quitting a job without a plan)

### Training Improvements
- Focused on relationship guidance (most sycophantic conversations in absolute terms)
- "Difficult advice" dataset: training Claude to give thoughtful, nuanced responses that challenge users when appropriate
- Goal: protect user wellbeing over providing agreeable responses
