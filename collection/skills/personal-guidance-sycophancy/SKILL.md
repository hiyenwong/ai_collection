# How People Ask Claude for Personal Guidance

Methodology from Anthropic research (Apr 30, 2026) studying how users seek personal guidance from AI and implications for sycophancy.

## Core Concept

Study of how users engage with Claude for personal advice, emotional support, and guidance — and how AI sycophancy (tendency to agree with users) manifests in these contexts.

## Key Dimensions

1. **Types of personal guidance sought**: emotional support, relationship advice, life decisions, career guidance
2. **Sycophancy risks**: AI models may agree with users rather than give honest advice
3. **Measurement methodology**: Systematic analysis of conversation patterns in personal guidance contexts
