# Key Papers on Quantum Number Theory

## Paper 1: Quantum algorithms for problems in number theory, algebraic geometry, and group theory
**arXiv**: 1206.6126v1
**Authors**: Wim van Dam, Yoshitaka Sasaki
**Published**: 2012-06-26

### Key Insights
- Review of quantum algorithms achieving superpolynomial speedup for algebraic problems
- Shor's algorithm as the canonical example (factoring integers)
- Hidden subgroup problem framework for discrete log, elliptic curve discrete log
- Quantum algorithms for algebraic geometry (point counting on varieties)
- Group theory problems amenable to quantum solutions

### Algorithm Categories
1. **Factoring family**: Shor's algorithm, period finding
2. **Discrete log family**: Shor's variant for multiplicative/group discrete log
3. **Algebraic geometry**: Quantum point counting
4. **Group theory**: Hidden subgroup problem

## Paper 2: A quantum number theory
**arXiv**: 2108.10145v1
**Authors**: Lucas Daiha, Roberto Rivelino
**Published**: 2021-08-18

### Key Concepts
- **q-numbers (quantum numbers)**: Operators in Hilbert space
- **c-numbers (classical numbers)**: Eigenvalues generated by q-number operators
- **2-component natural q-number**: N = (N₁, N₂), satisfying Heisenberg-Dirac algebra
- **3-component integer q-number**: Z = (Z₁, Z₂, Z₃), obeying Lie algebra structure
- **QNSV (Quantum Number State Vectors)**: Orthonormal basis sets for qu$n$its

### Framework
- Quantum number theory (QNT) as extension of classical number theory
- Probabilistic interpretation from operator representation
- Quantum mapping operation to relate different Hilbert subspaces
- Connection to quantum computing and high-dimensional computations

## Paper 3: Quantum Correlations and Number Theory
**arXiv**: 0202346v2
**Authors**: H. E. Boos, V. E. Korepin, Y. Nishiyama, M. Shiroishi
**Published**: 2002-02-20

### Key Results
- Emptiness formation probability P(n) in Heisenberg XXX antiferromagnet
- P(n) expressed in terms of Riemann zeta function with odd arguments
- Analytical formula for P(5) derived for first time
- Numerical verification via Density Matrix Renormalization Group
- Quantum Monte Carlo for finite temperature asymptotic behavior

### Number Theory Connection
- Statistical mechanics ↔ number theory link
- Riemann zeta function ζ(k) with odd k appears in correlation functions
- Rational coefficients in quantum correlation formulas
- ln 2 and zeta values combine in physical observables

## Paper 4: Number Theory in Quantum Physics: Minicharged Particles and PTE
**arXiv**: 2603.12320v1
**Authors**: Junseok Lee, Fuminobu Takahashi, Yu-Dai Tsai
**Published**: 2026-03-12

### Key Discovery
- Anomaly cancellation conditions = degree k=3 Prouhet-Tarry-Escott problem
- Hidden sector must contain at least 4 minicharged states
- Minimal ideal solutions produce near-degenerate doublet mass spectrum
- Discovery of one minicharged particle suggests partner state

### Number Theory Problem
**Prouhet-Tarry-Escott (PTE) Problem**: Find two sets {a₁, ..., a_n} and {b₁, ..., b_n}
such that for k = 1, 2, ..., m:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i^j = \sum_{i=1}^{n} b_i^j$$

Degree k=3 solution connects to quantum anomaly cancellation.

## Paper 5: Quantum Probabilistic Subroutines and Problems in Number Theory
**arXiv**: 9907020v2
**Authors**: A. Carlini, A. Hosoya
**Published**: 1999-07-06

### Quantum Algorithms
- Quantum version of classical probabilistic algorithms (Rabin-style)
- Grover's operator for quantum search
- Shor's Fourier transform for period extraction
- Brassard's counting algorithm

### Applications
1. **Primality testing**: Quantum probabilistic approach
2. **Prime number theorem**: Quantum algorithm for asymptotic analysis
3. **Goldbach conjecture**: Quantum counting for representations

### Features
- Full unitarity and reversibility
- Usable as subroutines in larger quantum networks
