# Deep Boltzmann Quantum States for Spin Glasses

Combining neural quantum states with Boltzmann machine architectures for frustrated quantum systems. Based on Leone, Dutta & Heyl (arXiv: 2605.15899).

## Core Methodology

- Deep Boltzmann Quantum States (DBQS): neural quantum states + Boltzmann machines
- Addresses classical and quantum spin glasses with energy frustration
- Efficiently represents exponentially large local energy minima landscape
- Handles disorder-induced frustration that breaks conventional approaches

## Architecture

1. Neural quantum state provides variational wavefunction ansatz
2. Boltzmann machine architecture captures complex correlations
3. Deep structure enables representation of multi-modal energy landscapes
4. Variational optimization finds ground states in frustrated systems

## When to Use

- Classical spin glass ground state problems
- Quantum spin glass simulation
- Frustrated quantum many-body systems
- Disordered systems with energy frustration
- Neural quantum state architectures for complex energy landscapes

## Key Advantages

1. Handles exponentially large local minima landscape
2. Combines representational power of neural quantum states with Boltzmann expressiveness
3. Effective for both classical and quantum spin glasses
4. Addresses limitations of conventional variational methods in frustrated systems
