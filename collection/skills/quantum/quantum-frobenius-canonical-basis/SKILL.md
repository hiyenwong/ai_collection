# Quantum Frobenius Morphism and Canonical Basis Compatibility

## Core Result

The quantum Frobenius morphism and its splitting are NOT fully compatible with the canonical basis for any finite-dimensional simple Lie algebra when rank is sufficiently large.

## Key Connections

1. **Incompatibility location**: Same place where Leclerc found his imaginary vectors
2. **Monomial cone issue**: Monomials in the tight monomial cone which do NOT belong to the canonical basis
3. **Rank dependence**: Incompatibility emerges at sufficiently high rank

## Applications

- Analyzing canonical basis stability under quantum Frobenius morphism
- Understanding tight monomial cone structure
- Identifying imaginary vectors in quantum group representations
