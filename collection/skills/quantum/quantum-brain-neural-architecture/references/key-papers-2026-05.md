# Key Papers — Quantum × Neuroscience Intersection

## 2505.01735 — Brain-Inspired Quantum Neural Architectures (QSNN+QLSTM)
**Authors:** Eva Andrés, Manuel Pegalajar Cuéllar, Gabriel Navarro (Univ. Granada)

**Core idea:** Two-stage model mirroring brain's hypothalamus + hippocampus for anomaly detection.
- Stage 1 (QSNN): Extracts low-level features, filters noise via spike firing probability
- Stage 2 (QLSTM): Captures correlated patterns, converts short-term to long-term memory
- Applied to credit card fraud detection; compared against classical models and QNNs
- Addresses imbalanced datasets, limited data, low data quality — quantum models find patterns classical models miss

**Architecture mapping:**
- Prefrontal cortex → QSNN (accumulates features, shapes context)
- Hippocampus → QLSTM (forms/retrieves specific memories)
- Atkinson-Shiffrin model: STS → LTS conversion via QLSTM temporal dynamics

## 2411.13378 — Quantum-Brain: Vision-Brain Understanding
**Core idea:** Quantum-inspired NN for vision-brain understanding using entanglement between brain voxels to enhance connectivity exploration. Combines brain signal connectivities with quantum entanglement properties. Self-attention replaced/augmented with entanglement measurement.

## 2507.10722 — Bridging Brains and Machines
**Core idea:** Position/survey paper on convergence of neuroscience, AGI, and neuromorphic computing. Framework grounded in brain physiology highlighting synaptic plasticity, sparse spike-based communication, and multimodal association as design principles for next-gen AGI.

## 2509.05919 — Brain-Inspired Scalable Quantum Vision
**Core idea:** "Forest Before Trees" cognition paradigm — classical NN analyzes global low-frequency information to guide targeted quantum circuits toward critical high-frequency image regions. Addresses scalability bottleneck of quantum vision.

## 2503.07681 — Transforming NNs into Neuromorphic Quantum Models
**Core idea:** Method to transform traditional neural networks into neuromorphic quantum models that mimic human brain functioning, usable on standard laptops (no quantum hardware required). Lowers barrier to quantum-inspired model creation.

## 2511.08493 — RL Control of Quantum Error Correction
**Core idea:** Uses reinforcement learning for adaptive QEC to handle environmental drift without halting computation. Key for fault-tolerant quantum computing at scale.

## 2504.05660 — Entangling Quantum Memories Over 420 km
**Core idea:** Demonstrates entanglement between atomic ensemble quantum memories over 420km fiber using quantum frequency conversion. Memory-memory entangling probability beats repeaterless channel capacity.

## 2510.16658 — Foundation AI Models in Neuroscience Survey
**Core idea:** Reviews large-scale AI models across five neuroscience domains:
1. Neuroimaging and data processing
2. Brain-computer interfaces and neural decoding
3. Clinical decision support and diagnosis
4. Computational neuroscience modeling
