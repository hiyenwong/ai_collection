# Born-Rule Quantum Phase Transitions

## Description
Framework for analyzing Dynamical Quantum Phase Transitions (DQPTs) through Born-rule statistical measurement dynamics. DQPTs occur when a quantum state exhibits nonanalytic changes in its return probability (Loschmidt echo) during time evolution.

## Activation Keywords
- dynamical quantum phase transition
- born rule quantum measurement
- loschmidt echo
- quantum fidelity dynamics
- quantum measurement statistics
- nonanalytic quantum dynamics

## Core Concepts

### 1. Loschmidt Echo
- G(t) = <psi(0)|psi(t)> = <psi(0)|e^{-iHt}|psi(0)>
- Rate function: g(t) = -(1/N) ln|G(t)|^2
- DQPTs occur at critical times where g(t) is nonanalytic

### 2. Born-Rule Statistical Dynamics
- Measurement outcomes follow Born rule: P(outcome) = |<outcome|psi>|^2
- Statistical ensemble of measurements reveals DQPT signatures
- Connection between measurement statistics and dynamical free energy

### 3. Detection Methods
- Interferometric measurement of Loschmidt echo
- Statistical analysis of return probability distributions
- Fisher zero analysis in complex time plane

## Key Signatures
- Nonanalytic cusps in rate function g(t)
- Topological changes in dynamical order parameter
- Dynamical vortices in parameter space
- Scaling behavior near critical times

## Applications
- Characterizing quantum quenches
- Probing entanglement phase transitions
- Benchmarking quantum simulators
- Studying measurement-induced criticality
