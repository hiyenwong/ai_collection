# Quantum Learning Privacy-Generalization Framework

Information-theoretic analysis framework connecting privacy, stability, and generalization in quantum learning algorithms.

## Core Theoretical Framework

### Privacy-Stability Equivalence
- Epsilon-quantum differentially private learning algorithms are stable
- Stability implies generalization in quantum learning
- Quantum mutual information bounds expected generalization error

### Information-Theoretic Generalization Bounds
1. **Upper bound**: Expected generalization error via quantum mutual information
2. **Lower bound**: Expected true loss relative to empirical loss
3. **ITA**: Information-Theoretic Admissibility for dishonest learning algorithms

## Usage Patterns

### Analyzing Quantum Privacy Guarantees
Use quantum differential privacy framework to evaluate whether a quantum learning
algorithm provides formal privacy guarantees.

### Proving Generalization Bounds
Apply quantum mutual information bounds to derive generalization guarantees
for quantum neural networks and variational quantum algorithms.

### Designing Privacy-Preserving Quantum ML
Combine epsilon-quantum DP with stability analysis to design quantum learning
systems with formal privacy protections.
