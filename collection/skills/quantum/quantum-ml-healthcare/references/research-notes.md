## Research Gaps Identified

1. Limited experimental validation of QNNs on real medical data
2. No clear demonstration of quantum advantage for healthcare tasks
3. Lack of standardized benchmarks for quantum healthcare ML
4. Insufficient work on explainability of quantum medical models

## Future Directions

1. Benchmark QNNs against classical baselines on medical datasets
2. Develop quantum-specific explainability methods for clinical use
3. Explore quantum advantage in high-dimensional genomics data
4. Build hybrid models that leverage quantum for specific sub-tasks
