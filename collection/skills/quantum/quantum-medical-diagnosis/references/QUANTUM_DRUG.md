# Quantum Drug Discovery Applications

## Overview

Quantum computing methods for pharmaceutical research and drug development.

## Drug Discovery Pipeline Stages

1. **Target Identification**: Quantum ML for protein structure
2. **Hit Discovery**: Quantum molecular screening
3. **Lead Optimization**: Quantum chemistry calculations
4. **Preclinical/Clinical**: Quantum trial design optimization

## Key Methods

### Quantum Chemistry
- **DFT (Density Functional Theory)**: Ground state calculations
- **HF (Hartree-Fock)**: Orbital approximations
- **QM/MM**: Hybrid quantum-molecular mechanics
- **VQE (Variational Quantum Eigensolver)**: Molecular energies

### Quantum Machine Learning
- **QSVM**: Quantum Support Vector Machine for screening
- **QNN**: Quantum Neural Networks for property prediction
- **QBM**: Quantum Boltzmann Machines

### HPQC Hybrid Architecture
- High-Performance Quantum Computing
- QPU-GPU hybrid systems
- Foundation models: FeNNx-Bio1
- Reference: arxiv 2603.17790

## Performance Metrics

| Method | Application | Classical | Quantum | Advantage |
|--------|-------------|-----------|---------|-----------|
| VQE | Binding energy | DFT | Faster | Accuracy |
| QSVM | Screening | ML | Better separation | Feature space |
| HPQC | Simulation | GPU | Quantum-accurate | Chemistry |

## Challenges

1. **Molecular complexity**: Large molecules exceed qubit capacity
2. **Error rates**: Chemistry calculations require precision
3. **Integration**: Classical-quantum workflow complexity
4. **Validation**: Clinical-level accuracy needed
