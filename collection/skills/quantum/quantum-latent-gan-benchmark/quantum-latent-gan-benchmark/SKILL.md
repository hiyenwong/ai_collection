# A Controlled Benchmark of Quantum-Latent GAN Augmentation for Brain MRI

## Summary
Controlled benchmark isolating quantum generator contribution to brain-MRI augmentation. Quantum and classical generators statistically indistinguishable. No augmentation variant outperforms real-data-only training. Low-data benefit behaves as regularization not faithful data expansion.
