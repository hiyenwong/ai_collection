## Core Concept
Learning and memory require a balance between plasticity and stability: synaptic connections must encode new information without collapsing, saturating, or erasing previously useful structure. Associative-memory models can appear to learn successfully when fixed background connectivity already carries part of the task, making it difficult to distinguish genuine recall dynamics from structural assistance.
