# Quantum Minimax Estimation of High-Order Functionals

## Description
Novel approach to the minimax estimation of high-order functionals from the perspective of quantum computing. Presents estimators for both classical functionals F_alpha(P) = sum p_i^alpha of discrete distributions and quantum functionals F_alpha(rho) = tr(rho^alpha) of mixed states. These functionals connect to Renyi entropy and Tsallis entropy.

## Key Concepts
- Minimax estimation of high-order functionals via quantum arguments
- Classical functional: F_alpha(P) = sum_{i=1}^S p_i^alpha for discrete distribution P
- Quantum functional: F_alpha(rho) = tr(rho^alpha) for mixed state rho
- Connection to Renyi entropy and Tsallis entropy
- Sample complexity advantages from quantum arguments

## Core Methodology
1. **Estimator Construction**: Build estimators for both classical and quantum functionals
2. **Quantum Arguments**: Leverage quantum computational arguments for minimax bounds
3. **Entropy Connection**: Relate estimated functionals to Renyi and Tsallis entropies
4. **Complexity Analysis**: Derive sample complexity for estimation to target accuracy

## Applications
- Quantum entropy estimation
- Statistical property estimation
- Distribution property testing
- Quantum state characterization

## Pitfalls
- alpha >> 1 regime has different behavior than alpha near 1
- Quantum and classical estimators may have different optimality conditions
- Mixed state estimation requires different techniques than pure state
