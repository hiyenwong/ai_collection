## Core Insight
Organizations cannot govern what they cannot see. Traditional compliance for deterministic web apps fails for AI systems that emerge without formal oversight. Telemetry-first governance is a categorical architectural shift.

## Key Methods
### Four Principles
1. **Proactive Discovery**: Automatically find undocumented AI systems
2. **Telemetry Evidence**: Replace manual attestation with automated probes
3. **Continuous Posture**: Always-on validation vs point-in-time audits
4. **Architecture-Backed Proof**: Structural metadata validation

### Zero-Trust Telemetry Boundary
- Ephemeral read-only probes
- Validate structural metadata without ingressing source code/PII
- No payload-level data exposure

### AI Observability Extractor Agent
- Scans LangSmith and Datadog LLM telemetry
- Automatically registers undocumented AI systems
- Shifts governance from self-report to machine observation
