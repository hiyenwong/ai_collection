# Hybrid Layer-Wise ANN-SNN With Surrogate Spike Encoding-Decoding Structure

## Abstract

We propose a novel hybrid ANN-SNN framework that integrates layer-wise encode-decode SNN blocks within conventional ANN pipelines. Central to our method is the use of surrogate gradients for a bit-plane-based spike encoding function, enabling end-to-end differentiable training across ANN and SNN layers. This design achieves competitive accuracy with energy-efficient computing.
