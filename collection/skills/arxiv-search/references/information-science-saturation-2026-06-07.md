1. From less common categories (cond-mat.mes-hall, etc.) that cross into the daily theme
2. Pure information theory papers without quantum content
3. Pure quantum papers without information science content

### Novel Paper Discovery Strategy
When saturation is high:
- Check papers from unusual categories that cross-list with the daily theme
- Look for papers that have methodology not yet captured in any skill
- Lower the score threshold but maintain the "no existing skill" requirement

1. cs.IT listing pages (primary for information theory papers)
2. quant-ph listing pages (primary for quantum physics papers)
