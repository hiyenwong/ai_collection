# Quantum Biological Neural Correlations

Quantum generative models for synthesizing biological neuronal activity data with spatial and temporal correlations. Based on Hernandes & Greplova (arXiv: 2409.09125).

## Core Approach

- Model spatial and temporal correlations of biological neuronal activity
- Quantum generative framework achieves reliable outcomes with fewer trainable parameters
- Captures both spatial (neuron-to-neuron) and temporal (time-evolution) correlation structure
- Potential for quantum advantage in neuroscience data generation

## When to Use

- Generating synthetic neural activity data with realistic correlation structure
- Comparing quantum vs classical generative model parameter efficiency
- Modeling biological neuronal population dynamics
- Reducing training data requirements for neuroscience ML pipelines
- Quantum advantage demonstration in neuroscientific applications

## Key Advantages

1. Fewer trainable parameters than classical generative models
2. Captures both spatial and temporal neuronal correlations
3. Potential quantum advantage for large-scale neural population modeling
4. Reduces data collection burden for neuroscience experiments
