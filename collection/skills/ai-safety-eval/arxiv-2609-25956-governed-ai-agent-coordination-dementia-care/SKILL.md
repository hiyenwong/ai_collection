# Governed AI-Agent Coordination for Dementia Care

**arXiv ID:** 2609.25956
**Categories:** cs.MA
**Utility Score:** 0.92

## Summary

This paper presents a governed multi-agent coordination framework for dementia care applications, focusing on architecture design, safety contracts, and evidence-derived workflow verification. The work addresses critical safety and governance requirements for deploying AI agents in healthcare settings.

## Key Contributions

1. **Governed Coordination**: Multi-agent coordination framework with built-in governance mechanisms
2. **Safety Contracts**: Formal safety contracts for healthcare AI agent deployments
3. **Workflow Verification**: Evidence-derived workflow verification for dementia care applications
4. **Healthcare Focus**: Specifically designed for dementia care with strict safety requirements

## Relevance to AI Systems

- **Healthcare AI**: Directly applicable to safe deployment of AI agents in healthcare
- **Safety Contracts**: Demonstrates formal approaches to agent safety in critical domains
- **Governance**: Provides governance framework for multi-agent healthcare systems
