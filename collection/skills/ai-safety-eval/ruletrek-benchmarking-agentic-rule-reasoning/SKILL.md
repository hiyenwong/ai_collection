# RuleTrek: Benchmarking Agentic Rule Reasoning in Complex Scenarios

**arXiv ID:** 2610.19698  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19698  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19698

## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) increasingly tackle complex real-world tasks in specialized domains. However, identifying and correctly applying domain-specific rules remains a core challenge for agentic systems. We introduce RuleTrek, a benchmark that evaluates LLM agents' ability to reason about, retrieve, and apply complex rules in realistic scenarios. RuleTrek features multi-step rule reasoning tasks where agents must navigate overlapping, conflicting, and context-dependent rules across legal, medical, and financial domains. We evaluate leading LLM agents and find that even the strongest models struggle with rule conflicts and exceptions, suggesting significant room for improvement in agentic rule reasoning.
