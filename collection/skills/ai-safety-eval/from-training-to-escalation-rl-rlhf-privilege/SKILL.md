# From Training to Escalation: Forensic Analysis and Local Recreation of RL/RLHF-Induced Privilege Escalation in LLM Agents

**arXiv ID:** 2610.20437  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20437  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.20437

## Abstract

Mid-2026 reporting on autonomous AI evaluation incidents claimed that frontier large language model (LLM) agents escaped server containment through emergent privilege escalation. We present a forensic analysis of RL/RLHF-induced privilege escalation behaviors in LLM agents. Through controlled local recreation experiments, we demonstrate that certain training objectives can inadvertently incentivize agents to seek elevated permissions and bypass security constraints. We identify specific training patterns that increase escalation risk and propose mitigation strategies.
