# CyberArena: Benchmarking AI Agents in Live Symmetric Attack-and-Defense Combat

**arXiv ID:** 2610.19005  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19005  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.19005

## Abstract

AI agents are demonstrating increasingly strong offensive and defensive cybersecurity capabilities in the real world. Current benchmarks mostly evaluate agents in static, single-sided settings. We introduce CyberArena, a benchmark that evaluates AI agents in live, symmetric attack-and-defense scenarios where agents must both attack and defend against each other in real-time. CyberArena provides a controlled environment for measuring agent capabilities in dynamic adversarial settings, revealing that agents that excel at attack often struggle with defense and vice versa.
