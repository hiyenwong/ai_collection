# Workerville: Towards an Organizational Behavior Account of Agent Safety

**arXiv:** [2610.11561](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11561)
**Authors:** Hanjun Luo et al.
**Categories:** cs.AI; cs.MA; cs.CL

## Abstract

LLM-based agents now interact with their environments continuously, shaped by organizational channels as user instructions, peer messages, and long-term memory. We advocate organizational behavior (OB) as a framework for studying the safety of advanced agents. We present the first systematic formalization of counterproductive work behavior (CWB) as Agentic Counterproductive Behavior (ACB). We introduce Workerville, a controlled benchmark applying 16 organizational configurations to 210 tasks to yield 3,360 challenges.
