# Medicine + Quantum Dual Role Pattern

## Key Insight (2026-06-11)

Quantum computing plays a dual role in medicine and healthcare:

### 1. Quantum as Accelerator (medical AI enhancement)

- **HQNN blood cell classification** (arXiv:2605.23324): ResNet-50 + quantum layers improve classification by +3.7% macro F1
- **Hybrid quantum FBPINN** (arXiv:2606.01110): Quantum circuits as enhanced subdomain representors in domain-decomposed PINN for complex PDE solving
- **Quantum medical image encoding** (arXiv:2505.06471): Fourier-based quantum encoding reduces gate requirements 4x+ for medical imaging
- **Hybrid QNN for breast cancer** (arXiv:2605.23324): Thermographic image classification with quantum feature amplification

### 2. Quantum as Protector (healthcare infrastructure security)

- **Post-quantum pharmacovigilance** (arXiv:2606.09412): ML-KEM-768 + ML-DSA-65 for securing drug safety data pipelines against harvest-now-decrypt-later attacks
- **Quantum tunneling PUF for IoMT** (arXiv:2605.22113): Quantum tunneling gate-leakage PUF for implantable medical device authentication (entropy 0.9999998, power 96nW/bit)
- **PQC IoT healthcare** (related): Post-quantum migration for connected medical devices

### Research Implication

When scanning for quantum + medicine papers, use both query sets:
- Acceleration: `quantum neural network medical imaging diagnosis healthcare`
- Security: `post-quantum cryptography pharmacovigilance IoMT device authentication medical`

The dual role is under-recognized in surveys — most focus on one side. Comprehensive quantum medicine research should cover both.
