# Hybrid Quantum Genetic Algorithm (HQGA) vs Classical GA

## Comparative Results

HQGA outperforms classical Genetic Algorithm in portfolio optimization:

1. **Faster Convergence**: Reaches optimal solution in fewer generations
2. **Higher Diversity**: Maintains population diversity longer
3. **Fewer Evaluations**: Requires fewer evaluations-to-solution than brute-force

## HQGA Design

- Quantum component enhances search space exploration
- Classical component handles fitness evaluation and selection
- Hybrid crossover leverages quantum superposition for diversity

## When to Use

- Portfolio optimization with many assets
- Problems where classical GA gets stuck in local optima
- When quantum hardware access is available
- Need for faster convergence with quality solutions
