# Persistent LLM Orchestration of Heterogeneous Robot Teams via Partial Delegation to Multi-Agent Protocols

**arXiv ID:** 2610.15224  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15224  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.15224

## Abstract

Natural language specifications let non-expert users task robot teams, and large language models (LLMs) have recently proven effective at grounding such specifications into executable plans. However, persistent orchestration of heterogeneous robot teams requires more than one-shot planning: the orchestrator must maintain state, handle failures, and coordinate across diverse robot capabilities over extended missions. We propose a persistent LLM orchestration framework that partially delegates low-level coordination to established multi-agent protocols while retaining high-level task understanding and adaptation. The LLM maintains a persistent memory of mission state, delegates routine coordination to protocol-based controllers, and intervenes when novel situations arise.
