# ExperienceIndex: Artifact-Grounded Memory

**arXiv:** [2610.10091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10091)
**Authors:** Peter Baile Chen et al.
**Categories:** cs.AI; cs.CL; cs.LG
**Utility Score:** 0.9

## Abstract

We introduce ExperienceIndex, a novel experience layer for AI agents that captures and reuses knowledge about artifacts based on prior reasoning traces. ExpIdx stores two complementary forms of experience: (i) single-artifact experiences that summarize an artifact contribution to prior tasks and (ii) artifact-pair experiences that encode structural relationships discovered during past reasoning. Across diverse corpora, ExpIdx delivers consistent gains, raising answer quality by up to 11.0 points and reducing online dollar cost by up to 50.5%.
