# Degrees of Freedom in Multi-Agent Coordination: Between Centralized and Decentralized Regimes

**arXiv ID:** 2610.17751  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17751  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.17751

## Abstract

Large language model (LLM)-based multi-agent systems (MAS) are commonly organized around two coordination regimes. Centralized coordination provides explicit control and global oversight, while decentralized coordination offers flexibility and robustness. We study the degrees of freedom available to agents operating between these regimes and propose a framework for understanding when and how to transition between centralized and decentralized coordination. Our analysis reveals that hybrid approaches that dynamically adjust the degree of centralization based on task complexity and agent capability outperform fixed-regime approaches.
