# Mental-Models for Multi-Agent Systems

**arXiv:** [2610.12453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12453)
**Utility:** 0.90

## Key Innovation

Explicit structured representations (mental models) for reasoning about what other agents know, intend, and are likely to do under partial observability.

## Problem

Current agentic systems operate through prompt design, memory, or end-to-end behavior cloning, lacking explicit models of other agents' knowledge and intentions. Robust multi-agent decision-making requires reasoning about other agents' mental states.

## Approach

Mental models as explicit data structures that capture:
- What other agents know (knowledge state)
- What other agents intend (goal inference)
- What other agents are likely to do (action prediction)

## Practical Use

- Improve multi-agent coordination in collaborative tasks
- Enable agents to reason about teammates' capabilities and knowledge
- Build more robust multi-agent systems under uncertainty
