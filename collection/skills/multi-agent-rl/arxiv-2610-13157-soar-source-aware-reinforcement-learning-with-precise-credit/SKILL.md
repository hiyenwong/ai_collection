# SOAR: Source-Aware Reinforcement Learning with Precise Credit Assignment for Search Agents

**arXiv:** [2610.13157](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13157)
**Authors:** Shengze Huang et al.
**Categories:** cs.CL; cs.AI; cs.LG
**Utility Score:** 0.9

## Abstract

Reinforcement learning (RL) for search agents often suffers from evidence fabrication. We propose SOAR (Source-Aware Reinforcement learning), a paradigm that transforms monolithic reasoning into source-explicit trajectories. SOAR disentangles external and internal information into parallel, observable streams via a Source-Aware Workflow, and incorporates Source-Consistent Reward to guide the policy evolution. Evaluations show that SOAR outperforms baselines on 3B and 7B backbones by 7.3% and 5.4%, while reducing evidence fabrication by 74.8%.
