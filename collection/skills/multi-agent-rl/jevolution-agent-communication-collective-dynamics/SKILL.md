# Jevolution: A Framework for Studying Communication and Collective Dynamics in AI Agents

**arXiv ID:** 2610.14449  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14449  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.14449

## Abstract

Experiments with remote decision-making agents depend on more than the policy: the world may change during inference, the simulator determines which actions are available, and births and immigration alter who can act. We present Jevolution, an instrumented spatial predator-prey framework that makes these environmental factors first-class objects of study. Agents operate in a 2D grid world where they must hunt prey, avoid predators, and communicate with peers. The framework supports systematic variation of communication protocols, population dynamics (births, deaths, immigration), and action space constraints. We demonstrate that collective behavior emerges from the interaction between individual policies and environmental structure, and that communication protocols co-evolve with population dynamics.
