## Core Concept
This paper describes an automatic method for adjusting or tuning models of multi-agent motion. Simulating the motion of bird flocks, human crowds, vehicle traffic, and other multi-agent systems is a widely used technique. These simulations model the behavior of a single group member (bird, human, or vehicle). The group behaviors (flock, crowd, traffic) emerge from interactions between group members.
