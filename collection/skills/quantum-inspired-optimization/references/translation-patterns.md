# Quantum-to-Classical Translation Patterns

## Pattern 1: Superposition to Probability Distribution

Quantum: |psi> = sum(alpha_i |i>)
Classical: P(i) = |alpha_i|^2

## Pattern 2: Quantum Interference to Gradient

Quantum: Interference amplifies good solutions
Classical: Gradient descent on continuous relaxation

## Pattern 3: Entanglement to Correlation

Quantum: Entangled qubits share state
Classical: Correlated variables in optimization

## Pattern 4: Quantum Tunneling to Thermal Jumps

Quantum: Tunneling through barriers
Classical: Simulated annealing with temperature schedule

## When Translation Works Best

- Optimization problems with smooth landscapes
- Problems where quantum advantage is primarily from superposition
- Cases where quantum noise degrades performance
- Large-scale problems exceeding quantum hardware capacity
