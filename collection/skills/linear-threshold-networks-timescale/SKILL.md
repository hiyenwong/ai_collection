# Timescale Limits of Linear-Threshold Networks

## Overview

Advances the study of global asymptotic stability in Linear-Threshold Networks (LTNs) with asymmetric neural interactions and heterogeneous dissipation under the structural Lyapunov Diagonal Stability (LDS) condition.

## Key Contributions

1. **One-parameter LTN family**: Preserves LDS condition with parameter-independent equilibrium set
2. **Fast limit**: Converges to a Projected Dynamical System (PDS) — globally exponentially stable under LDS
3. **Slow limit**: Converges to a discontinuous Hard-Selector System (HSS) — globally asymptotically stable under LDS
4. **Structural insight**: Resolving stability at fast and slow endpoints provides path toward global stability for biologically plausible LTNs

## Technical Framework

- **LTNs**: Capture mesoscale behavior of interacting neural populations
- **LDS condition**: Structural Lyapunov condition ensuring diagonal stability
- **Timescale separation**: Single parameter controls fast/slow regime
- **Limiting systems**: PDS (fast) and HSS (slow) capture essential stability mechanisms

## Relevance

- **Computational neuroscience**: Neural population dynamics modeling
- **Control theory**: Stability analysis of switched/piecewise-linear systems
- **Neuromorphic engineering**: Circuit-level dynamics of neural hardware
