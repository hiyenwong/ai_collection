# Provable Quantum Advantage for Dynamical Phase Transitions

## Description

Methodology from arXiv:2606.30396 (Jun 29, 2026) — Proves that estimating dynamical quantum phase transitions (DQPTs) to certain precision is intractable even for quantum computers, while deciding a subsystem variant is as hard as simulating generic quantum circuits, implying provable exponential quantum advantage. Provides quadratically faster quantum algorithm for searching critical times of local DQPTs with Heisenberg-limited precision.
