# De-Individualizing fMRI Signals via Mahalanobis Whitening and Bures Geometry

## Summary
Uses Mahalanobis data whitening to distill meaningful information from fMRI signals. Interprets whitening as two-stage de-individualization motivated by Bures distance, connected to quantum mechanics. Potential for improving Alzheimer's diagnosis accuracy.
