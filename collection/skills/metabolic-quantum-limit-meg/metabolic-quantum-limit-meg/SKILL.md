# Metabolic quantum limit to the information capacity of magnetoencephalography

## Summary
Combines energy resolution limit of magnetic sensing with metabolic power to derive technology-independent bound on MEG information capacity. Bound factorizes into geometry, metabolism, and Planck's constant, giving max 2.2 Mbit/s for human brain. Shows quantum-limited noise floor constrains observable complexity of noninvasive brain imaging.
