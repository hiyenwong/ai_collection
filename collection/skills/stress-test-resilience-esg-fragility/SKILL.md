# ESG and Joint Fragility in Equity Markets

## Context

Analysis of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) factors' relationship with joint fragility in equity markets under stress conditions.

## Core Methodology

1. **Define joint fragility metrics** for equity portfolios
2. **Integrate ESG scores** as resilience factors
3. **Stress test** under various market scenarios
4. **Measure amplification effects** of ESG on portfolio resilience

## Implementation Steps

1. Collect ESG ratings and market data
2. Construct joint fragility measures
3. Run stress amplification analysis
4. Validate results across market regimes

## Pitfalls

- ESG data quality varies significantly across providers
- Joint fragility requires careful copula modeling
- Stress scenarios must be economically meaningful

## Verification

- Cross-validate with historical crisis periods
- Compare against traditional risk metrics
