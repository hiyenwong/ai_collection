## Paper Abstract

> Throughout the literature on Neural Cellular Automata (NCAs), it is often taken for granted that the systems learn attractors. This is shown through evolving the system for many timesteps and noting visual similarity to the goal state. There remain many questions after such an analysis. Namely, what kind of attractors do we have? Is their behavior ordered or chaotic? Can we estimate stability over very long time horizons? What really happens in the attractor when perturbations are applied?
