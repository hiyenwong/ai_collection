## Abstract
This paper presents Ag/Hf₀.₅Zr₀.₅O₂(HZO) memristive artificial neurons capable of multiple spiking functionalities: Time-to-First-Spike (TTFS), spike count coding, and firing rate coding. Using a two-step annealing method to optimize memristor parameters, the device achieves LIF neuron behavior without additional electronic overhead.

## Key Contributions
1. Multi-mode spiking neuron using Ag/HZO memristor
2. Two-step annealing process for parameter optimization
3. Minimal circuit design (memristor + resistor only)
