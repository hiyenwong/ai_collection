# Thermocoherent Framework for Neural Information Flow

## Paper
- **Title**: The physical basis of information flow in neural matter: a thermocoherent perspective on cognitive dynamics
- **Author**: Onur Pusuluk
- **Date**: April 5, 2026

## Overview
A multiscale resource-theoretical framework that specifies the physical basis of information flow in living neural matter, connecting cognitive dynamics to thermodynamics and coherence properties.

### Thermocoherent Perspective
- Information flow analyzed through **resource theory** lens

### Key Insights
1. **Physical basis** of cognition specified through thermodynamics
2. **Resource-theoretic** quantification of neural information
3. **Multiscale integration** from molecules to networks
4. **Coherence** as a computational resource in neural systems
