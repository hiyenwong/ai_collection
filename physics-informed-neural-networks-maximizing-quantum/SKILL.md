# Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Maximizing Quantum Fisher Information in Time-Dependent Many-Body Systems

## Overview

Quantum Fisher Information (QFI) sets the ultimate precision limit for parameter estimation and is therefore a central quantity in quantum metrology. In time-dependent many-body systems, however, maximizing QFI is a highly non-trivial task due to the combined effects of non-commutativity, control complexity, and the exponential growth of the Hilbert space.
